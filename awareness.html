♻️ Waste Awareness & Segregation Guide

Why Segregate Waste?

Proper waste segregation reduces landfill usage, prevents environmental pollution, and allows recyclable materials to be processed efficiently. By separating your waste at the source, you contribute to a cleaner, greener planet.

Green Bin (Wet Waste)
  • Vegetable peels
  • Fruit scraps
  • Cooking oil
  • Tea leaves & coffee grounds
  • Leftover food
Blue Bin (Dry Waste)
  • Paper & Cardboard
  • Plastic bottles & containers
  • Metal cans
  • Glass bottles
  • Clean packaging materials
Red Bin (Hazardous)
  • Batteries
  • Medical waste (syringes, bandages)
  • Sanitary items
  • E-waste (electronics)
  • Chemical containers
